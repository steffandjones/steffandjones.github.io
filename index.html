About

I graduated from Harvard University in May 2025 with an SB in Mechanical Engineering. During my time there, I balanced rigorous academics with competing as a Varsity Track & Field athlete, training over 25 hours per week.

As Aerodynamics Co-Lead for Harvard's first FSAE team, I developed the aerodynamic package that improved downforce and handling stability. I also served as Head Course Assistant for Heat Transfer and contributed to building the Ivy League's first liquid bi-propellant rocket.

I've competed internationally as a 400m sprinter (46.7s), which taught me discipline, resilience, and the ability to perform under pressure, qualities I bring to every engineering challenge.
