About

I graduated from Harvard University in May 2025 with an SB in Mechanical Engineering. While there, I balanced my studies with Varsity Track & Field, training over 25 hours a week for four years. I also worked as the Head Course Assistant for ES183: Introduction to Heat Transfer and joined the Harvard Automotive Society and Rocket Propulsion Group, helping build Harvard’s first FSAE car and the Ivy League’s first liquid bi-propellant rocket.

I’ve competed in Track & Field for over a decade, focusing on the 400m (46.7s) and representing my country internationally. The sport has taught me discipline, patience, and how to keep going when things don’t go as planned.

At Harvard, I loved the freedom to explore - taking courses in Python, Electrical Engineering, and Semiconductors, as well as in Greek Mythology, Chinese Philosophy, and Irish Folklore.

Outside the classroom, I’m a music lover, tennis player, and social person who enjoys time with friends, concerts, and exploring Boston. Moving from the countryside to the city has shaped who I am, and I’ll always be grateful for my time at Harvard.
