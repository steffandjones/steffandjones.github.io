I graduated from Harvard University in May 2025 with an SB in Mechanical Engineering. During my time there, I balanced rigorous academics with competing as a Varsity Track & Field athlete — training over 25 hours per week while leading aerodynamics for Harvard's first FSAE car, serving as Head Course Assistant for Heat Transfer, and contributing to the Ivy League's first liquid bi-propellant rocket.

I've interned at Aston Martin Lagonda and Alesi Surgical, run a tutoring practice, and am currently Venture & Product Development Manager at EQL Tech — while building two apps on the side.

Represented Wales internationally as a 400m sprinter (46.7s PB). The discipline and resilience of elite sport shapes how I approach every engineering challenge.
