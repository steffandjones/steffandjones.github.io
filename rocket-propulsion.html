Harvard Rocket Propulsion

Ivy League's First Liquid Bi-Propellant Rocket

Project Overview

Member of the Harvard Rocket Propulsion Group, contributing to the design and development of the Ivy League's first liquid bi-propellant rocket engine. This ambitious project combined thermodynamics, fluid mechanics, and materials engineering to create a functional rocket propulsion system.

Key Achievements

  • Contributed to Ivy League's first liquid rocket engine
  • Designed and tested propulsion system components
  • Performed thermal and structural analysis
  • Assisted with test stand instrumentation
  • Participated in static fire testing procedures

Technical Details

The project involved designing a regeneratively-cooled combustion chamber, developing injector geometry for optimal fuel-oxidizer mixing, and creating feed systems with proper flow control. Extensive use of CFD and FEA ensured safe operation under extreme temperatures and pressures. Safety protocols and testing procedures were developed in accordance with aerospace standards.
