Role Overview

Served as Founder's Associate at EQL Tech, working directly with the founding team to drive business operations, strategic initiatives, and partnership development. This multifaceted role combined business strategy, operations management, and cross-functional coordination in a fast-paced startup environment.

The position required wearing multiple hats, from market research and competitive analysis to project management and stakeholder coordination, providing exposure to all aspects of building and scaling a technology venture.

Key Responsibilities

  • Business operations and strategic planning support
  • Market research and competitive landscape analysis
  • Team coordination and cross-functional project management
  • Partnership development and stakeholder engagement
  • Process optimization and operational efficiency improvements
  • Strategic initiative execution and progress tracking
  • Supporting founder with high-priority business decisions

Impact & Contributions

Strategy: Conducted market research and competitive analysis to inform product positioning and go-to-market strategy. Synthesized industry trends and customer insights to support strategic decision-making at the founding team level.

Operations: Managed cross-functional projects, coordinated team activities, and ensured alignment across workstreams. Implemented processes to improve operational efficiency and team productivity in a fast-moving startup environment.

Partnerships: Supported business development through stakeholder engagement, partnership exploration, and relationship management. Helped identify and evaluate strategic opportunities for growth and collaboration.
