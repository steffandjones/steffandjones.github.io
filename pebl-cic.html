PEBL-CIC

Product Design Engineer / Software Engineering

Role Overview

Product Design Engineer and Software Engineering role at PEBL CIC, a social enterprise developing innovative technology solutions for agricultural and environmental monitoring. Based in Swansea, Wales, PEBL creates tools that support farmers and coastal communities through smart sensing technology.

The role focused on firmware and software development in preparation for product launch in 2026, working on the GrowProbe and SubCam systems—embedded devices for environmental data collection and monitoring.

Key Responsibilities

  • Writing and testing firmware for sensors, logging, and communications
  • Developing low-level software for data capture, storage, and transfer
  • Testing and debugging electronics in laboratory and field environments
  • Improving system reliability, power efficiency, and usability
  • Translating field feedback into firmware and software improvements
  • Integrating electronics into product housings and mounts
  • Supporting mechanical design changes for sensors and connectors

Technical Work

The position involved embedded systems development with a focus on reliability and field deployment. Work included writing firmware for microcontrollers, implementing communication protocols, optimizing power consumption for battery-operated devices, and ensuring robust data logging under challenging environmental conditions.

Collaboration with the product design and engineering team was essential to integrate electronic systems into weatherproof housings, balance technical constraints with user requirements, and iterate rapidly based on real-world testing feedback.

This role bridged software engineering, embedded systems, and product development—requiring both technical depth and the ability to work across disciplines to deliver functional, field-ready devices.
