About the Business

Steffan Jones Tutoring provides personalized academic tutoring and university guidance for students at GCSE, A-Level, and university level. As a Harvard-educated Mechanical Engineer from Wales, I'm passionate about making education accessible and helping students from underrepresented backgrounds achieve their academic dreams.

I started this tutoring business because I experienced firsthand the limited support available to Welsh students aiming for top institutions. Being the only Welsh student at Harvard made me realize how important it is for students to have guidance from someone who understands their journey. I want students from Wales and beyond to know that the highest heights are achievable, and they don't have to figure it all out alone.

Services Offered

  • Academic Tutoring: Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Computer Science, English, and more at GCSE, A-Level, and university level
  • University Guidance: Complete support through the application process—from finding schools to crafting essays and preparing for interviews
  • SAT/ACT Preparation: Comprehensive test prep for US university admissions
  • Interview Preparation: Specialized coaching for Oxford, Cambridge, MIT, and other elite university interviews
  • Harvard Admissions Interviewing: Official Harvard interviewer with insider knowledge of what committees look for
  • Bilingual Support: Sessions available in both English and Welsh

Teaching Philosophy

My tutoring style is patient, clear, and flexible. I start by understanding each student's strengths and gaps, then focus our time where it will make the biggest difference. I aim to make sessions engaging and supportive while pushing students to believe in themselves and strive higher than they thought possible.

At Harvard, I served as Lead Course Assistant for Thermodynamics and tutored Life Sciences, where I discovered my genuine passion for teaching. I've worked with students of all ages and backgrounds, including children with additional learning needs, which taught me how to adapt to different learning styles and meet students where they are.
